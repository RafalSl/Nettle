Shape, space, motion

  • Pills and circles: radius 999. Panels 26–30, sheets 34–40.
  • Screen margin 20; gaps 8 inside a group, 14–18 between groups.
  • Targets 44 pt or larger; rows 52–56 pt.
  • Nothing touches a screen edge: tab bar, sheets and Save float.
  • Motion only on first load: sections rise in with a 0.1 s stagger, the ring draws in, dots pop in. The aurora drifts slowly. No other animation.

Buttons

Primary: one per screen, the form Save floats at the bottom. Glass: everything else. Outline: a secondary action that should stand out. Danger: destructive, at the end of a screen; Delete solid only inside its confirmation sheet.

Chips

Selected: accent with a tick. Unselected: outline. Suggested by the app: dashed accent, never selected until tapped. Editable item: glass with a remove cross. Add: dashed outline.

Gestures (each has a tap alternative)

  • Swipe the day ring: previous or next day (faint arrows).
  • Tap a dot on the ring: that entry’s Edit or Delete sheet (the log row).
  • Swipe a log row left: Delete, then the confirmation sheet (tap the row).
  • Long-press a quick action: its full form (“Other or earlier…”).
  • Swipe down on a form: Cancel; with changes, Discard Changes first.
  • Later (UX-13): press and drag around the ring to pick a time.
